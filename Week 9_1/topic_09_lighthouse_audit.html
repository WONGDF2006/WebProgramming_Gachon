Lighthouse accessibility audit

How to run it

  1. Open your page in Chrome and press F12 (or right-click → Inspect).
  2. Open the Lighthouse tab (it may be under »).
  3. Tick only Accessibility.
  4. Click Analyze page load and wait 10–20 seconds.
  5. Expand each failing item to see the exact element and how to fix it.

Reading the score

90–100 · GreenAutomated checks pass. Still test by keyboard and screen reader.
50–89 · OrangeNeeds work. Most fixes are quick: alt text, contrast, labels.
0–49 · RedSome users can't use the site. Fix missing alt, missing labels, and low contrast first.

100 does not mean fully accessible. Lighthouse can't tell if alt text is good (only that it exists), and can't find illogical reading order, keyboard traps, or confusing language.

Key points

  • Aim for 90+. Each failing item links to the exact element in the DOM.
  • Quick wins: missing alt text, low contrast, missing form labels, buttons with no text.
  • Test manually with NVDA, VoiceOver, or JAWS as well.
