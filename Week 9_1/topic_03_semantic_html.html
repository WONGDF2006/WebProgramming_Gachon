  <nav aria-label="Main navigation">
    <a href="/">Home</a>
  </nav>
</header>
<main>
  <article><h1>Hello</h1></article>
</main>
<footer>© 2026</footer>

What the screen reader announces

ElementAnnounced as
<header>"banner" landmark (page header)
<nav>"navigation" landmark — users jump straight here
<main>"main" landmark — the skip-link target
<article>"article" — makes sense on its own
<aside>"complementary" landmark
<footer>"contentinfo" landmark (page footer)
<section>"region" landmark, when it has an aria-label
<h1>–<h6>"Heading level 1: …" — users skim by heading

Key points

  • One <main> and one <h1> per page.
  • Don't skip heading levels — style sizes with CSS instead.
