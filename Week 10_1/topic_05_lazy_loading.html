<!-- ✓ Hero image: load it first -->
<img src="hero.webp" alt="Hero" fetchpriority="high" width="1200" height="600">
✓ Lazy load
  • Galleries below the fold
  • Images in long articles
  • YouTube and map embeds
✗ Don't lazy load
  • Hero / banner image
  • Logo in the header
  • Anything visible on load

Open DevTools → Network → Img, reload, then scroll down. The photos below download only as they come near the screen.
