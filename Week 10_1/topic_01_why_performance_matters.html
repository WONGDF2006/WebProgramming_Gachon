Why performance matters

53%of mobile users leave if a page takes over 3 s (Google, 2018)
7%fewer conversions per 1 s of delay
+1%revenue per 100 ms faster (Walmart, 2012)

Performance also affects SEO

Since 2021, Google uses the Core Web Vitals as ranking signals:

  • LCP (Largest Contentful Paint): how fast the biggest element appears.
  • CLS (Cumulative Layout Shift): does the page jump around while loading?
  • INP (Interaction to Next Paint): how fast the page responds to clicks.

Key points

  • Every second of delay costs users and conversions.
  • Slow pages rank lower in search results.
  • Users on 3G/4G and older devices feel slowness the most.
