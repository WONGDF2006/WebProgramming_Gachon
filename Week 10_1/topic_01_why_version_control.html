Why version control?

You change index.html, something breaks, and you can't remember what you deleted. Version control solves this by recording every change, so you can undo mistakes, read the history, and work with others safely.

The "filename nightmare" without it

project.html
project_v2.html
project_FINAL.html
project_FINAL_v2.html
project_FINAL_USE_THIS.html
project_ACTUALLY_FINAL.html

Which one is the real final version? Who changed what? What if two people edit at once?

📜 Track history

Every change: what, who, when.

👥 Collaborate

Several people edit without overwriting each other.

⏪ Undo mistakes

Go back to any earlier version.

Key points

  • Version control tracks what changed, who changed it, and when.
  • It replaces copies like file_v2_FINAL with one searchable history.
  • You can undo any mistake, even from weeks ago.
  • Teams can work on the same files at the same time.
