What is Accessibility?

People use the web in very different ways. Your site has to work for all of them.

👁️ Visual impairment

Min-jun uses a screen reader (NVDA) that reads the page aloud. He moves with Tab. If your button is a <div>, he can't reach it.

🤝 Motor disability

Soyeon uses a switch device, one button that cycles through focusable elements. She needs large targets and a logical tab order.

🧠 Cognitive differences

Hyunwoo has dyslexia. He needs clear fonts, generous spacing, simple language, and no flashing animations.

👂 Hearing impairment

Yuna is Deaf. She needs captions on every video and a visual version of every sound cue.

Accessibility helps everyone. Curb cuts were made for wheelchairs but help strollers and bikes too. Captions help in noisy places. Good contrast helps in bright sunlight.

Key points

  • A11y covers visual, motor, cognitive, and hearing disabilities.
  • Improvements for disabled users make the site better for all users.
  • In many countries, inaccessible government and commercial sites are illegal.
