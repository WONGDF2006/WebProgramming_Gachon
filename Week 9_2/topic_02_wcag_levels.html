WCAG Levels

AMinimum — required

Without these, some people can't use the site at all. Alt text, form labels, no colour-only meaning.

AAStandard target — expected

Legally required in many places. 4.5:1 contrast, text resizable to 200%, full keyboard access.

AAAExcellent — enhanced

For key features. 7:1 contrast, sign language for video, simpler reading level.

Key points

  • The four principles are POUR: Perceivable, Operable, Understandable, Robust.
  • WCAG 2.1 is the current standard; WCAG 2.2 adds more focus-related rules.
