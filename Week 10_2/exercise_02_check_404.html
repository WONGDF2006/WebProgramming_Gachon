E2 · Find and understand a 404 page

Visit a URL that doesn't exist on the site:

$ curl -s -o /dev/null -w "%{http_code}" https://wongdf2006.github.io/WebProgramming_Gachon/this-does-not-exist.html
404

What this site showed (before exercise 3)

GitHub's generic page, titled "Page not found · GitHub Pages". It has GitHub's branding, no link back to the course, and nothing that tells the visitor they're on the right site.

Is that a good experience?

Not really. A good 404 page looks like the rest of the site, says clearly that the page is missing, and gives a way back home. GitHub Pages will serve 404.html automatically if the repo has one. That's exercise 3.
